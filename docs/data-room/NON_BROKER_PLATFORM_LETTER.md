## Purpose

This letter states, for investor diligence and for the company’s data room, the permanent regulatory and commercial identity of Clear Path Markets Science Corp. and ClearPath Trader. It restates commitments already published on clearpathtrader.com and in product terms: we are a **data, analytics, education, and API-key platform**. We are **not** a brokerage, and we will **not** become one.

## What ClearPath is

ClearPath Trader is software that:

- Displays market data and charts (stocks, forex, crypto, commodities) sourced through vendor APIs and keys held server-side;
- Provides analytical tools, education, encyclopedias, and research desks;
- Charges for software memberships (for example via Stripe), not for custody, execution, or investment advice.

The company’s product is the screen, the methodology made measurable, and the educational layer around it — not a seat on an exchange and not a customer brokerage account.

## What ClearPath is not — and will not become

Clear Path Markets Science Corp. and ClearPath Trader are **not**, and **will not become**:

1. A broker-dealer (including under FINRA / SEC broker-dealer registration);
2. A futures commission merchant;
3. A registered investment adviser;
4. A securities exchange or alternative trading system operated by ClearPath;
5. A bank, money services business, or custodian of customer funds or securities;
6. A signal service whose product identity is “buy / sell / hold” directives;
7. An **introducing broker (IB)**, finder for brokerages, or recipient of broker referral / IB kickbacks or soft-dollar arrangements tied to routing customers into brokerage accounts.

**FINRA:** Because ClearPath is not a broker-dealer and does not hold customer securities accounts, ClearPath is not a FINRA member firm and does not maintain a FINRA broker-dealer compliance pack. Optional pass-through connectivity (below) does not change that identity.

## Optional pass-through broker connect (and what it is not)

Users may optionally OAuth-link an account they already hold at a **licensed third-party broker** (for example Alpaca). In that configuration:

- The **licensed broker** performs KYC/AML, holds funds, and executes orders;
- ClearPath may transmit instructions the user explicitly submits to that broker (pass-through);
- Without a connected broker, ClearPath remains analytics and education only.

Pass-through is **not**:

- ClearPath acting as the broker;
- ClearPath introducing the user to a broker for compensation;
- ClearPath operating IB links, affiliate broker signup funnels, or residual payouts for opening brokerage accounts;
- ClearPath custodying money or guaranteeing fills.

This company will **never** adopt introducing-broker economics, broker referral commissions, or IB deep links as part of the business model.

## Permanence

This posture is intentional and permanent for Clear Path Markets Science Corp. and ClearPath Trader:

- We sell and operate a **platform of data, analytics, education, and API integrations**;
- We do **not** sell brokerage, custody, or personalized investment advice;
- We do **not** seek FINRA broker-dealer membership as a product roadmap item;
- We do **not** plan IB, introducing-broker, or broker-kickback revenue.

Any future optional connectivity to additional licensed brokers will remain pass-through only and will not convert ClearPath into a broker or an IB.
