### FINRA — explicit answer

**ClearPath does not have anything to do with FINRA membership as a broker-dealer.**

FINRA appears on the FTFC fintech checklist for companies that *are* broker-dealers or similarly regulated. ClearPath Trader is a **data / analytics / education / API platform**. We do not custody funds, do not execute as a broker, and do not give personalized investment advice. Optional Alpaca (or similar) OAuth is **pass-through** to a licensed broker; that broker does KYC/AML and execution.

See also: `NON_BROKER_PLATFORM_LETTER.md` (permanent policy).

## 7. Permanent non-broker / no-IB posture (summary)

Full letter: `NON_BROKER_PLATFORM_LETTER.md` · Live: `/technology-opportunity#non-broker-policy`

1. We are a **data, analytics, education, and API-key platform**.
2. We are **not** and **will not become** a broker-dealer, FCM, RIA, exchange, bank, MSB, custodian, or signal shop.
3. We are **not** an introducing broker and will **never** use IB links or broker referral kickbacks.
