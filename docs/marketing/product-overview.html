Why it exists

Financial education should adapt to people — not the other way around.

Most trading software is designed to keep people clicking. ClearPath Trader was built so the chart stays the center of attention, and the rest of the desk can be parked until you ask for it.

The manifesto

Some people see patterns. Some people need structure. Some people learn visually. Some people become overwhelmed by cluttered screens, endless indicators, and information overload. Before risking capital, understand the system. Before following signals, understand the logic. Before chasing markets, understand the relationships.

Founder question

“What if I built the trading platform my own brain actually needed?”

Richard A. Floyd · Founder & CEO

Built from need

Autism, ADHD, and TBI shaped the product. Flashing widgets and stacked alerts were not “engagement.” They were exhaustion. The durable asset is the terminal — not a personal brand, not casino hype.

What this is not

  • Not a broker. No order tickets. No customer funds.
  • Not financial advice or trade signals.
  • Not a website chatbot that books appointments.
  • Not aiclearpath.com — different company.

Intelligence · honesty protocol

If a vendor cell is missing, it stays blank.

ClearPath does not invent COT, IV, GEX, Level II, or timed CPI/NFP rows. Labels tell you the source. “DATA UNAVAILABLE” is a feature.

News wire

Live headlines on desks. Rate-limits are labeled as the vendor (e.g. NewsData), not as a COT failure.

Positioning / COT

Official CFTC.gov via /api/cot/history. Example map: XAUUSD → GC → 088691. Chips: CACHED · CFTC FETCH · NO CFTC MAP · PROVIDER ERROR.

Macro / FRED

Policy and inflation series when a FRED key is configured on the server. Client-supplied keys are rejected.

Economic wire

Calendar panels are a headline wire — not a fabricated timed-release grid. Empty beats stay empty.
