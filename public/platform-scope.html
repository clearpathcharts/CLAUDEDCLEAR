Clear Path Markets ScienceTrader — Platform Scope & Regulatory Position

1. Platform Classification

Clear Path Markets ScienceTrader is a read-only financial data visualization platform designed to improve accessibility and legibility of market data.

It functions solely as a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) interface for viewing and organizing financial information.

2. What Clear Path Markets ScienceTrader DOES

  • Displays financial market data from third-party providers
  • Applies visual formatting (color, layout, contrast)
  • Enhances readability and accessibility
  • Allows users to input symbols manually
  • Provides a structured data viewing environment

3. What Clear Path Markets ScienceTrader DOES NOT DO

Clear Path Markets ScienceTrader is a passive, non-transactional system.
  • Does NOT execute trades
  • Does NOT place or complete orders
  • Does NOT route or transmit trade data
  • Does NOT connect to brokers for execution
  • Does NOT provide investment advice or recommendations
  • Does NOT suggest buying, selling, or holding assets
  • Does NOT manage accounts or portfolios
  • Does NOT hold funds or assets
  • Does NOT prioritize or rank securities
  • Does NOT display “top movers” or “trending” assets
  • Does NOT generate alerts or trading prompts
  • Does NOT receive transaction-based compensation

4. Neutral Interface Statement

Clear Path Markets ScienceTrader operates as a neutral data visualization interface.

Visual formatting is applied uniformly and is not designed to influence trading decisions or emphasize specific securities.

5. Separation From Trading Activity

Clear Path Markets ScienceTrader has no connection to trade execution systems.

All trading activity occurs independently outside of the platform.

6. No Investment Advisory Relationship

Use of this platform does not create any advisory or fiduciary relationship.

7. Data Disclaimer

Data is sourced from third-party providers and may be delayed, inaccurate, or incomplete.

8. Risk Disclosure

Financial markets involve risk. Users should not rely solely on displayed data for decision-making.

9. User Responsibility

All financial decisions are made independently by the user.

Clear Path Markets ScienceTrader is a professional financial data platform. It is not a social network, dating service, or communication platform of any kind.

Users are strictly prohibited from engaging in any form of inappropriate, abusive, or unlawful behavior, including but not limited to:

  • Sending, transmitting, or attempting to distribute explicit, lewd, or pornographic content
  • Using the platform for dating, solicitation, or personal interactions unrelated to its intended purpose
  • Harassment, intimidation, or targeting of any individual or group

Clear Path Markets ScienceTrader maintains a zero-tolerance policy for such behavior.

Any violation will result in immediate termination of access without warning.

Clear Path Markets ScienceTrader reserves the right to:

  • Remove any content or user access associated with prohibited behavior
  • Preserve and document relevant activity for security and enforcement purposes
  • Report unlawful conduct to appropriate law enforcement or regulatory authorities where applicable

By using this platform, you agree to use it solely for its intended purpose as a financial data visualization tool.

Misuse of the platform may violate applicable laws related to harassment, electronic communications, or distribution of unlawful content.

Clear Path Markets ScienceTrader is a passive visualization tool — a window into financial data, not a mechanism for trading or decision-making.
