Real World: Groceries

Supermarket Impact

Inflation causes input commodity costs (like wheat, milk, and packaging transport) to swell, forcing supermarkets to mark up retail prices of daily groceries.

Real World: Housing

Property Squeezes

When values scale upward rapid-fire, land appraisals and lease cost bands multiply, requiring renters to work longer hours to cover rent budgets.

Wages vs Expenses

If salaries remain fixed while retail products increase 8.5% year-over-year, employees experience a structural drop in real disposable wages.
