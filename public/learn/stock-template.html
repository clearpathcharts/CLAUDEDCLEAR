What is Market Capitalization?

The total dollar market value of a company's outstanding shares of stock, calculated by multiplying outstanding shares by the current share price.

What is Price-to-Earnings (P/E)?

The ratio for valuing a company that measures its current share price relative to its aggregate per-share earnings (EPS).
