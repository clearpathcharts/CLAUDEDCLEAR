Privacy Policy

User-provided API keys are used only to fetch market data and are not used for trading or transmitted to third parties.

3. No Financial Data Storage

The platform does not store brokerage credentials or financial account information.

4. Cookies

Cookies may be used to improve user experience.

5. Third-Party Data

Market data is provided by third-party APIs. We are not responsible for their accuracy or privacy practices.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect user data but cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated periodically.

8. ClearPath CPMS Android App

The ClearPath CPMS mobile application (package com.clearpath.cpms) may access the internet to stream educational video and third-party live news broadcasts (HLS). If you sign in, Firebase Authentication may store your email address and user ID to sync your profile and authorized media cabinet features. We do not sell personal data.

Live TV streams are provided by third-party broadcasters; their privacy practices apply to those streams. The app does not access brokerage credentials or execute trades.
