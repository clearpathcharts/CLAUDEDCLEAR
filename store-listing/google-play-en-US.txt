FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters)
--------------------------------------
ClearPath CPMS is the mobile command center for Clear Path Markets Science — one app with two powerful experiences:

📺 CPMS TV — Live News & Broadcast Directory
Watch live HLS news and finance streams from a curated global channel directory. Browse by category, search stations, and launch the full-screen theatre player. Includes business news, world news, weather, and documentary feeds.

🎬 CPMS Library — Premium Media Archive
Stream educational finance and market-intelligence videos organized by category: Finance TV, Indicator TV, Trading Anarchy TV, Market News TV, and Documentary TV. Built-in HLS playback for adaptive live streams and on-demand content.

✓ One install — both CPMS TV and CPMS Media Library
✓ Seamless navigation between the main ClearPath terminal and TV modes
✓ Founder media cabinet for authorized content publishing (Firebase)
✓ Designed for traders, researchers, and macro-focused learners
